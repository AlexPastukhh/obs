## Screenshot-backed transcript

This region maps common account operations to Identity services.

- `UserManager<TUser>` creates users, manages passwords, claims, roles and generated tokens.
- `SignInManager<TUser>` performs password sign-in, sign-out and external-login orchestration.
- `RoleManager<TRole>` creates and manages roles.
- `IEmailSender` is an application-provided delivery abstraction used by the default UI or by custom flows; Identity generates confirmation/reset tokens but does not magically send mail.
- Register an `IEmailSender` implementation, generate email-confirmation or password-reset tokens, place them in a properly encoded link, then call `ConfirmEmailAsync` or `ResetPasswordAsync`.
- Common controller operations include finding users, checking passwords, adding/removing roles and claims, and reading the current principal’s claims.
