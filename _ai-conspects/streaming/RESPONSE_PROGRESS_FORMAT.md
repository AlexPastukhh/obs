# Response Progress Format

Use this compact map at the end of work responses.

```text
Done:
- What was just done.
- What it means for the overall goal.
- Status of the block after this.

Now:
- Where we are now.
- What must be checked/committed before continuing.

Next:
- Next concrete step.
- What it means conceptually.
- Which sources/images it should close.
- What remains pending after it.

Later:
- Next 2-4 steps after that.
- What each step means.
```
