## Future behavior algorithms

### Chemical fire / no-fuel algorithm

Idea:
- thoughts, cravings, stimulation pull, and absence of desire for useful work should not be treated as identity;
- they may be treated as chemical / nervous-system events;
- feeding stimulation or thought loops increases their inertia;
- feeding useful work increases useful inertia.

Status:
- important, but not designed as a workflow yet.

### Silence / thought-stop algorithm

Idea:
- notice thought noise;
- do not debate with it;
- do not feed it;
- create silence;
- return to next physical action.

Status:
- future workflow / future operating rule.

### Return-to-rails algorithm

Idea:
- after drift, do not only log the event;
- name the state;
- stop fuel;
- return to `Today State.md`;
- choose one physical next action.

Status:
- future workflow candidate.
