## Rule

`📉📈 Fast recovery after slip` is not a score reward by itself. It is recovery context that can prevent a slip from becoming a penalty event.

`👁️🚫🥊🎭➡️🕳️ No-resistance known drift` is a fundamental penalty candidate and should not be hidden inside F0/Koff only.
